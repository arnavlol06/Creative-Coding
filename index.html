Order From Chaos

1Idea

The project essentially focuses on how complex patterns can emerge from a simple set of rules. Its based off the wolfram method. I want to check out the relationship between order, randomness and repetition. The main question is how much complexity can emerge from a system that focuses on only a few instructions.

2System and Method

The work will be generated using p5.js in which each cell changes state according to its left neighbour, itself, and its right neighbour.

Ill experiment with pj5 with varying patterns and observe the different outputs from the same system.

3References

Stephen Wolfram — A New Kind of Science

His research into cellular automata demonstrates how simple rules can generate complex and unpredictable behaviour. This forms the conceptual foundation of my project.

Vera Molnár — Algorithmic and generative drawings

Her systematic use of geometric rules and controlled variations inspires me to explore how small changes in parameters can create different visual outcomes.

Casey Reas — Process

His exploration of rule-based generative art inspires my approach to translating computational instructions into evolving visual compositions.

4Proposed Physical Artifact

I propose creating a layered generative artwork that translates the patterns produced in p5.js into a physical object.

The physical form is still open to experimentation. I want to investigate whether the pattern works best as a layered sculpture, a cut-out structure, or a generative print.

The artifact I want to try and create would be something related to a layered light piece. It will essentially create the patterns and randomness from pj5 into a physical object. When stacked these layers will create an overlapping geometric pattern. (still experimenting with ideas.
